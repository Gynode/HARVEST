# HARVEST User Guide

> **Status:** there is no HARVEST wallet, explorer or sidechain to use yet. This guide describes how they are
> intended to work — read it as specification. See `AGENTS.md` at the repository root for what actually exists.

Welcome to HARVEST! This guide will help you understand how to interact with the HARVEST blockchain, manage your HARVEST (HRV) coins, and handle your NFTs.

## 1. Understanding HARVEST

HARVEST is a sidechain built on the Cardano ecosystem, designed for fast, efficient, and secure transactions. It uses a Proof-of-Authority (PoA) consensus mechanism, which means transactions are validated by a set of trusted Node Handlers (Masters), ensuring network stability and speed.

## 2. Managing Your HRV Coins

Your HARVEST (HRV) coins are the native currency of the HARVEST blockchain. You can use them for transaction fees, participate in future governance, and for various in-app purchases or rewards within the HARVEST ecosystem.

### 2.1. Checking Your HRV Balance

To check your HRV balance, you will typically use the HARVEST Wallet application or the HARVEST Blockchain Explorer.

#### Using the HARVEST Wallet:

1.  Open the HARVEST Wallet application.
2.  Your current HRV balance will be displayed prominently on the main screen.

#### Using the HARVEST Blockchain Explorer:

1.  Navigate to the HARVEST Blockchain Explorer website (URL to be provided).
2.  Enter your HARVEST wallet address in the search bar.
3.  The explorer will display your balance, along with your transaction history.

### 2.2. Sending HRV

To send HRV to another address, use the HARVEST Wallet application.

1.  Open the HARVEST Wallet application.
2.  Select the "Send" or "Transfer" option.
3.  Enter the recipient's HARVEST wallet address.
4.  Enter the amount of HRV you wish to send.
5.  Confirm the transaction details. A small transaction fee in HRV will be applied.
6.  Approve the transaction (you may need to enter your password or confirm with a hardware wallet).

### 2.3. Receiving HRV

To receive HRV, simply provide your HARVEST wallet address to the sender. Your address can be found in your HARVEST Wallet application, usually by selecting a "Receive" or "Deposit" option.

## 3. Managing Your NFTs

HARVEST supports the minting and transfer of Non-Fungible Tokens (NFTs). Your 3125 unique NFTs will be managed on this sidechain, allowing for efficient and low-cost transactions.

### 3.1. Viewing Your NFTs

You can view your NFT collection using the HARVEST Wallet application or the HARVEST Blockchain Explorer.

#### Using the HARVEST Wallet:

1.  Open the HARVEST Wallet application.
2.  Navigate to the "NFTs" or "Collectibles" section.
3.  Your owned NFTs will be displayed, often with their associated images and metadata.

#### Using the HARVEST Blockchain Explorer:

1.  Navigate to the HARVEST Blockchain Explorer website (URL to be provided).
2.  Enter your HARVEST wallet address in the search bar.
3.  Look for a section displaying your owned NFTs, which will list their unique IDs and potentially links to their metadata.

### 3.2. Transferring NFTs

To send an NFT to another address, use the HARVEST Wallet application.

1.  Open the HARVEST Wallet application.
2.  Go to your "NFTs" or "Collectibles" section.
3.  Select the NFT you wish to transfer.
4.  Choose the "Send" or "Transfer" option.
5.  Enter the recipient's HARVEST wallet address.
6.  Confirm the transaction details. A small transaction fee in HRV will be applied.
7.  Approve the transaction.

## 4. Exploring the HARVEST Blockchain

The HARVEST Blockchain Explorer is a public tool that allows anyone to view the activity on the HARVEST network. It's useful for:

*   **Checking Transaction Status**: See if your transactions have been confirmed.
*   **Viewing Blocks**: Explore the details of each block added to the blockchain.
*   **Monitoring Network Activity**: Get an overview of the total transactions, active Node Handlers, and network health.

To use the explorer, simply visit the HARVEST Blockchain Explorer website (URL to be provided) and use the search bar or navigation options to find the information you need.

## 5. Security Tips

*   **Protect Your Private Keys/Seed Phrase**: Never share your private keys or seed phrase with anyone. These are the ultimate control over your funds and NFTs.
*   **Use Strong Passwords**: For any wallet or service, use unique, complex passwords.
*   **Be Wary of Phishing**: Always double-check URLs and sender addresses to avoid malicious websites or emails.
*   **Keep Software Updated**: Ensure your HARVEST Wallet and any other related software are always up to date to benefit from the latest security patches.

For further assistance or to report issues, please refer to the official HARVEST support channels.

# Governance

The HARVEST DAO governs the project's treasury and parameters.

## Proposals

A proposal moves through a defined lifecycle: drafted, submitted, reviewed by Node Handlers, then put to a
vote, and finally executed or cancelled.

Node Handler review is a gate — a proposal that fails review does not reach a community vote.

## Voting

Voting is **quadratic**, so holding far more HRV does not translate into a proportionally larger say.
Proposals are subject to a quorum and a threshold.

Voting power is denominated in HRV rather than in currency, which means governance does not depend on the
treasury holding anything. Delegation is supported: holders may delegate their voting power to another address.

## The treasury

Treasury spending requires multiple approvals, and critical changes are subject to a timelock.

**The treasury is not funded.** No contributions have been made and no funding has been secured, so there is
nothing to allocate today. It is intended to be funded in fiat, and funding is being sourced — no source is
named. The target is 2027.

## Status

The governance rules are specified but not implemented. They exist today as a Python specification; the
on-chain validators are to be written in Aiken. Nothing here is running on Cardano yet — see
[Project status](/docs/status).
